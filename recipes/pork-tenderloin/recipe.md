---
title: Roasted Pork Tenderloin with Apple Cider Reduction
description: Herb-crusted pork tenderloin roasted to juicy perfection, sliced and drizzled with a glossy apple cider pan sauce--served with caramelized roasted root vegetables and beet greens.
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 45
estimatedCost: 28
ingredients:
  - 2 pork tenderloins (~2-2 1/2 lbs total)
  - 2 Tbsp olive oil
  - 1 Tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  - 1 Tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup pure apple cider
  - 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  - 2 Tbsp cold unsalted butter
  - 4-5 medium parsnips, peeled, cut into chunks
  - 3-4 medium turnips, peeled, cut into wedges
  - 4-5 medium beets with tops, peeled and cut into wedges
  - 2 medium onions, cut into wedges
  - Beet greens (washed, torn)
  - 3-4 Tbsp olive oil (for veggies)
  - 2 tsp fresh rosemary (for veggies)
  - 2 tsp fresh thyme (for veggies)
  - Salt and black pepper
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 425F. Line sheet pan with foil.
  - Toss parsnips, turnips, beets, onions with 3 Tbsp olive oil, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper. Roast 25 minutes.
  - Add torn beet greens (drizzle with remaining oil + salt). Toss; roast 15-20 minutes more until caramelized.
  - Pat pork dry. Rub with 2 Tbsp olive oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic, salt, pepper.
  - Sear pork in hot skillet 2-3 min/side. Transfer to oven; roast 12-18 min until 140-145F internal. Rest 10 min.
  - In same skillet, simmer apple cider + Dijon 4-6 min until reduced. Swirl in cold butter until glossy.
  - Slice pork; serve with roasted vegetables and cider sauce spooned over.
tips:
  - Sheet-pan veggies + one skillet pork = easy cleanup.
  - Resting pork ensures juiciness.
---
