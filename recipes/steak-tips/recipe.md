---
title: One-Pan Garlic Butter Steak Tips with Mushrooms & Asparagus
description: Seared steak tips tossed in garlic-herb butter with golden mushrooms and crisp asparagus—all in one skillet, finished with crusty bread for sopping up the sauce.
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 35
estimatedCost: 28
ingredients:
  - 1½–2 lbs beef sirloin or ribeye tips
  - 1–1½ lbs sliced baby bella mushrooms
  - 1–1½ lbs asparagus, trimmed
  - 6–8 Tbsp unsalted butter (divided)
  - 4–6 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 Tbsp fresh thyme or rosemary, chopped
  - "Optional: ½–1 tsp red pepper flakes"
  - Juice of ½ lemon (optional)
  - 1 loaf crusty bread, sliced
  - Salt, black pepper, garlic powder (optional)
  - 2 Tbsp olive oil
instructions:
  - Pat steak tips dry. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  - Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in large skillet over medium-high. Sear steak tips 2–3 min/side in batches. Remove.
  - Add 2 Tbsp butter; cook mushrooms 8–10 min until golden.
  - Push mushrooms aside; add asparagus + 2 Tbsp butter. Cook 4–6 min until crisp-tender.
  - Add remaining butter, garlic, herbs, red pepper flakes. Stir 1–2 min.
  - Return steak + juices; toss to coat. Squeeze lemon over if using.
  - Serve with crusty bread for dipping sauce.
tips:
  - One skillet = minimal cleanup.
  - Mushrooms add deep earthiness.
---
