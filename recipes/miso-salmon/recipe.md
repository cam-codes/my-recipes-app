---
title: Sheet-Pan Miso-Glazed Salmon with Bok Choy & Sweet Potatoes
description: Salmon fillets glazed with sweet-savory miso (or soy-honey sub), roasted with caramelized sweet potatoes and tender-crisp bok choy—served with fluffy jasmine rice.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 30
estimatedCost: 30
ingredients:
  - 1½–2 lbs salmon fillets (4 portions)
  - ¼–⅓ cup white miso paste (or soy sauce sub)
  - 2–3 Tbsp honey or brown sugar
  - 2–3 Tbsp soy sauce
  - 2–3 Tbsp mirin or rice vinegar
  - 1–2 tsp sesame oil
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 3 large sweet potatoes, cut into chunks
  - 4–6 heads regular bok choy, halved/quartered
  - 3–4 Tbsp olive oil
  - Salt and black pepper
  - 1½–2 cups uncooked jasmine rice (for side)
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 425°F. Line sheet pan with foil.
  - "Whisk glaze: miso (or sub), honey, soy, mirin, sesame oil, garlic."
  - Brush half glaze on salmon; set aside.
  - Toss sweet potato chunks and quartered bok choy with olive oil, salt, pepper. Spread on pan.
  - Roast 15 minutes.
  - Add salmon to pan; brush with remaining glaze. Roast 8–12 minutes until salmon flakes.
  - "Meanwhile, cook jasmine rice: boil water, stir in rice, cover, simmer 15 minutes."
  - Serve salmon with roasted veggies and rice.
tips:
  - Quarter regular bok choy for even roasting.
  - Soy sub works well if no miso.
