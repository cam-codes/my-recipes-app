---
title: Giant Cast Iron Chocolate Chip Cookie
description: Thick, chewy skillet cookie with crispy edges and gooey center-baked in cast iron for pizzeria-style perfection, served warm with ice cream or plain.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 25
estimatedCost: 10
ingredients:
  - 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  - 3/4 cup packed light brown sugar
  - 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  - 1 large egg + 1 egg yolk
  - 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  - 2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - 1/2 tsp salt
  - 1 1/2 cups semi-sweet or bittersweet chocolate chips (or chopped chocolate)
  - Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans
  - Optional: flaky sea salt for topping
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 350F. Place 10-12 inch cast iron skillet in oven while preheating.
  - Whisk melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth.
  - Add egg, egg yolk, and vanilla; whisk until thick and glossy.
  - In separate bowl, whisk flour, baking soda, and salt.
  - Stir dry ingredients into wet until just combined.
  - Fold in chocolate chips (and nuts if using).
  - Remove hot skillet from oven (use mitts!). Add 1-2 tsp butter if pan isn't well-seasoned.
  - Press dough evenly into skillet. Sprinkle flaky sea salt on top if desired.
  - Bake 20-25 minutes until edges are deep golden and center is still slightly soft.
  - Cool in skillet 10-15 minutes. Slice into wedges or scoop.
tips:
  - Underbake slightly for gooey center-it firms as it cools.
  - Make dough ahead and refrigerate up to 24 hours (add 2-4 min bake time if cold).
  - Serve warm with vanilla ice cream for ultimate indulgence.
