---
title: Elevated Chili Con Carne
description: Deep, layered chili with ancho-guajillo paste, beef chuck + ground beef, dark beer, chocolate, and cinnamon—topped with crème fraîche, cheddar, avocado, and cilantro for bold, complex flavor.
prepTime: 25
cookTime: 120
estimatedCost: 26
ingredients:
  - 1 lb ground beef (80/20)
  - 1 lb beef chuck, cubed
  - 2 medium onions, finely diced
  - 5–6 cloves garlic, minced
  - 3–4 dried ancho chilies
  - 3–4 dried guajillo chilies
  - 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  - 2 (14-oz) cans fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  - 1 (15-oz) can kidney beans, drained
  - 1 (15-oz) can black beans, drained
  - 1 cup dark beer or brewed coffee
  - 4–5 cups low-sodium beef stock
  - 2 tsp ground cumin
  - 2 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  - 1–2 tsp red pepper flakes or cayenne
  - 1–2 oz dark chocolate (70%+)
  - Olive oil
  - Salt and black pepper
  - "Toppings: crème fraîche, shredded cheddar, diced avocado, chopped cilantro, lime wedges"
instructions:
  - Toast dried chilies in dry skillet 1–2 min/side. Soak in 2 cups boiling water 20–30 min. Blend with liquid into paste.
  - Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in Dutch oven. Brown cubed chuck in batches; remove. Brown ground beef; remove.
  - Cook onions + salt 6–8 min until soft/golden. Add garlic + spices 1 min. Stir in tomato paste 2–3 min.
  - Return meat. Add chili paste, tomatoes, beer/coffee, 4 cups stock. Simmer low, partially covered, 1½–2 hours.
  - "**Last 30 min:** add beans + chopped chocolate. Adjust thickness with more stock if needed."
  - Serve in bowls with desired toppings.
tips:
  - Make ahead — flavors improve overnight.
  - "**Pressure cooker version:** high 50 min + natural release 15–20 min."
