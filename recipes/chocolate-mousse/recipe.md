---
title: Silky Dark Chocolate Mousse
description: Rich, airy dark chocolate mousse with a subtle espresso note for depth, served in glasses with sweetened vanilla whipped cream and fresh berries—luxurious yet simple no-bake elegance.
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 0
estimatedCost: 15
ingredients:
  - 8 oz good-quality dark chocolate (70%+ cacao), chopped
  - 4 large eggs, separated
  - 1/4–1/3 cup granulated sugar (to taste)
  - 1 tsp instant espresso powder (optional, enhances chocolate)
  - 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  - Pinch salt
  - 1 1/2 cups heavy cream (cold – 1 cup for mousse, 1/2 cup for topping)
  - 1–2 Tbsp powdered sugar (for whipped cream topping)
  - 1/2 tsp vanilla extract (for whipped cream topping)
  - Fresh berries (raspberries, strawberries, or blackberries – 1–2 pints for garnish)
instructions:
  - Melt chocolate with espresso powder and 2 Tbsp water (double boiler or microwave in 30-sec bursts). Cool slightly.
  - Whisk egg yolks, sugar, vanilla, and salt into melted chocolate until smooth.
  - Whip 1 cup cold heavy cream to soft peaks. Gently fold into chocolate mixture.
  - Whip egg whites to stiff peaks. Gently fold into chocolate-cream mixture until no streaks remain.
  - Divide into 4 glasses. Chill at least 1–2 hours (or up to 2 days).
  - Before serving: Whip remaining 1/2 cup cream with powdered sugar and vanilla to soft peaks. Dollop on mousse and top with fresh berries.
tips:
  - Chill bowl and beaters for faster whipping.
  - For lighter sweetness, start with 1/4 cup sugar in mousse.
  - Make ahead—flavors deepen overnight.
