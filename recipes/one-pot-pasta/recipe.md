---
title: One-Pot Pasta with Tomato Sauce
description: Super-easy one-pot spaghetti where ground chicken, mushrooms, and marinara cook together with the pasta—no draining, minimal cleanup, big family-friendly flavor.
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 20
estimatedCost: 15
ingredients:
  - 1 lb ground chicken (or beef/turkey)
  - 8–12 oz dry spaghetti (broken in half if needed)
  - 1–2 cups sliced mushrooms
  - 1 jar (24–28 oz) marinara sauce or crushed tomatoes
  - 4 cups water (or chicken broth)
  - 1 onion, diced (optional)
  - 2–3 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
  - Salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes to taste
  - "Optional: grated Parmesan, fresh basil, spinach"
instructions:
  - In large pot over medium-high, brown ground chicken with onion and garlic (if using), breaking it up. Season.
  - Add mushrooms; cook 2–3 min until softened.
  - Pour in marinara and water/broth; stir and bring to boil.
  - Add spaghetti, pressing to submerge. Reduce to medium-low, cover, simmer 10–15 min (stir occasionally) until pasta is al dente and liquid mostly absorbed.
  - Adjust seasoning; stir in extras like cheese or herbs.
  - Serve hot.
tips:
  - Stir frequently to prevent sticking.
  - Add splash more liquid if drying out before pasta is done.
  - Great for minimal cleanup.
---
