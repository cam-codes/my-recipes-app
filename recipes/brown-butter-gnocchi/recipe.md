---
title: Crispy Gnocchi with Brown Butter Sage & Burst Tomatoes
description: Store-bought gnocchi pan-fried until golden and crisp, tossed in nutty brown butter with sage, garlic, and burst cherry tomatoes—quick one-pan comfort with bright, fresh flavor.
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 25
estimatedCost: 15
ingredients:
  - 20–24 oz (1.25–1.5 lbs) store-bought potato gnocchi
  - 6–8 Tbsp unsalted butter (divided)
  - 15–20 fresh sage leaves
  - 1.5–2 pints cherry tomatoes (~18–24 oz)
  - 3–4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced
  - ½–¾ cup freshly grated Parmesan (plus extra for serving)
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - Optional: pinch red pepper flakes
  - Optional: fresh basil or parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  - Optional: squeeze of lemon (for brightness)
instructions:
  - "Bring large pot of salted water to boil (optional par-boil if gnocchi are dense)."
  - "Heat 2–3 Tbsp butter in large skillet over medium-high until foaming. Add gnocchi in single layer (batches if needed)."
  - "Cook undisturbed 3–4 min until golden. Flip and cook another 3–4 min until crisp. Remove to plate."
  - "Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining 4–5 Tbsp butter. Once melted, add sage leaves and garlic."
  - "Cook 2–3 min, swirling, until butter turns golden brown and nutty, sage crisps, garlic fragrant."
  - "Add cherry tomatoes, salt, pepper, red pepper flakes (if using). Cook 3–5 min until softened and burst."
  - "Return gnocchi to skillet. Toss to coat in brown butter sauce."
  - "Remove from heat. Stir in grated Parmesan until melted. Adjust seasoning."
  - "Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and optional herbs/lemon."
tips:
  - Skip par-boiling for maximum crispiness—most store-bought gnocchi crisp directly from package.
  - Watch brown butter closely to avoid burning.
  - Make-ahead: Crisp gnocchi and make sauce; reheat gently with splash of water/stock.
