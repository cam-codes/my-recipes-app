---
title: Kimchi Chicken Stir-Fry
description: Quick, spicy Korean-inspired stir-fry with tender chicken, tangy kimchi, onions, and a punchy sauce--ready in under 30 minutes and great over rice.
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 15
estimatedCost: 18
ingredients:
  - 1-1 1/2 lbs boneless chicken thighs or breasts, sliced thin
  - 1-2 cups kimchi (chopped, plus some juice)
  - 1 onion, sliced
  - 2-3 green onions, chopped
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 Tbsp gochujang (optional for extra heat)
  - 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  - 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
  - "Optional: 1 tsp sugar, sesame seeds for garnish"
  - "Serve with: rice"
instructions:
  - Heat vegetable oil in large skillet or wok over medium-high.
  - Add chicken; stir-fry until mostly cooked (~5 min).
  - Add onion and garlic; cook 2-3 min until softened.
  - Stir in kimchi (with juice), gochujang, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar (if using).
  - Cook 4-6 min until chicken is fully done and sauce thickens slightly.
  - Stir in green onions at the end.
  - Serve hot over rice; garnish with sesame seeds.
tips:
  - Adjust kimchi amount for tang/spice level.
  - Use well-fermented kimchi for best flavor.
---
