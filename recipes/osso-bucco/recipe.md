---
title: Osso Buco alla Milanese (Beef Shank Version)
description: Tender braised beef shanks in white wine-tomato sauce, served over saffron risotto and topped with bright gremolata—classic Italian comfort with rich marrow flavor.
prepTime: 30
cookTime: 120
estimatedCost: 40
ingredients:
  - 4 bone-in beef shanks (4–5 lbs total)
  - Salt and pepper
  - All-purpose flour (~½ cup)
  - 3 Tbsp olive oil
  - 1 medium onion, diced
  - 2 medium carrots, diced
  - 1 celery stalk, diced
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup dry white wine
  - 1 (14-oz) can diced tomatoes
  - 1½–2 cups beef or chicken stock
  - Fresh thyme and rosemary sprigs
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 1½ cups arborio rice (for risotto)
  - 4–5 cups hot chicken stock (for risotto)
  - ½ cup dry white wine (for risotto)
  - 1 small onion, diced (for risotto)
  - 2 Tbsp butter + 2 Tbsp olive oil (for risotto)
  - Pinch saffron threads
  - ¾ cup grated Parmesan
  - ½ cup chopped parsley
  - Zest of 1 lemon
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced (for gremolata)
instructions:
  - Season and flour shanks. Sear in oil in pressure cooker sauté mode; remove.
  - Sauté onion, carrots, celery, garlic. Deglaze with wine.
  - Add tomatoes, stock, herbs. Nestle shanks. Pressure cook high 50 min + natural release 15–20 min.
  - Reduce sauce on sauté 10–15 min.
  - "Make risotto: Sauté onion, toast rice, add wine, then stock + saffron gradually, stirring 18–20 min. Finish with Parmesan."
  - Mix gremolata (parsley, lemon zest, garlic).
  - Serve shank on risotto, sauce over, gremolata sprinkled.
tips:
  - Beef needs longer than veal. Pressure cooker cuts time dramatically.
---
