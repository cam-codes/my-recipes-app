---
title: Crock Pot Oxtails
description: Fall-off-the-bone tender oxtails simmered low and slow in a rich, savory beef-tomato broth with carrots, celery, and herbs—perfect over mashed potatoes for ultimate comfort.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 480
estimatedCost: 35
ingredients:
  - 3–4 lbs oxtails
  - Salt and black pepper
  - 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
  - 1 large onion, chopped
  - 3 carrots, chopped
  - 3 celery stalks, chopped
  - 4 garlic cloves, minced
  - 2 cups beef broth
  - 1 cup red wine (optional, or more broth)
  - 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  - 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  - 2 bay leaves
  - 1 tsp dried thyme
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - "Optional: 1–2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce"
instructions:
  - Season oxtails with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high; brown oxtails on all sides (in batches if needed). Transfer to crock pot.
  - In the same skillet, sauté onion, carrots, celery ~5 min. Add garlic; cook 1 min.
  - Stir in tomato paste; cook 1–2 min. Deglaze with wine (if using), scraping bits.
  - Pour mixture over oxtails in crock pot. Add broth, tomatoes, bay leaves, thyme, paprika, Worcestershire.
  - Cover; cook on low 8–10 hours (or high 4–6 hours) until meat is fall-off-the-bone tender.
  - "Optional: Skim fat; thicken sauce on stove if desired."
tips:
  - Serve over mashed potatoes, rice, or polenta.
  - Low and slow is key for tender oxtails.
---
