---
title: Spaghetti with Meat Sauce
description: A rich, umami-packed meat sauce with red wine, anchovy depth, carrot sweetness, and a butter finish—tossed with al dente spaghetti for classic comfort elevated.
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 45
estimatedCost: 22
ingredients:
  - 1 lb dry spaghetti
  - 1 lb ground beef (85/15 or 80/20)
  - ½ lb ground pork
  - 1 medium onion, finely diced
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 small carrot, finely grated
  - 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 cup dry red wine
  - 1 (28-oz) can San Marzano whole tomatoes, hand-crushed
  - 1 tsp anchovy paste
  - 1 bay leaf
  - ½ tsp dried oregano
  - ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  - Small handful fresh basil, torn
  - 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
  - Olive oil
  - Salt and black pepper
  - Parmesan cheese, for serving
instructions:
  - Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in a large Dutch oven or deep sauté pan over medium-high. Brown beef and pork in batches, breaking up meat. Remove and set aside, leaving fat in pan.
  - Add onion, carrot, and a pinch of salt. Cook 5 minutes until softened.
  - Add garlic and anchovy paste; cook 1 minute.
  - Stir in tomato paste; cook 2–3 minutes until brick-red.
  - Pour in red wine, scrape bottom, and reduce by half (2–3 minutes).
  - Add hand-crushed tomatoes, bay leaf, oregano, red pepper flakes, and browned meat. Simmer low 25–35 minutes.
  - Meanwhile, cook spaghetti in heavily salted boiling water 1–2 minutes shy of package directions. Reserve 1 cup pasta water; drain.
  - Remove bay leaf from sauce. Stir in butter and torn basil.
  - Add drained pasta directly to sauce pot. Toss over low heat, adding reserved pasta water as needed for silky texture.
  - Serve with generous grated Parmesan.
tips:
  - One-pot sauce + one-pot pasta = minimal cleanup.
  - Use bronze-die pasta for better sauce cling.
---
