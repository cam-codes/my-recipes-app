---
title: Creamy Tuscan Chicken with Sun-Dried Tomatoes and Spinach
description: Juicy chicken cutlets in a garlicky cream sauce bursting with sun-dried tomatoes and wilted spinach, finished with Parmesan--bright, creamy, and served with roasted asparagus.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 35
estimatedCost: 25
ingredients:
  - 4 boneless chicken breasts (~2 lbs total)
  - Salt and black pepper
  - All-purpose flour (~1/4 cup for dredging)
  - 3 Tbsp olive oil
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup heavy cream
  - 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken stock
  - 3/4 cup grated Parmesan
  - 7-8 oz sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed, drained, chopped)
  - 4-5 oz baby spinach
  - "Optional: pinch red pepper flakes"
  - 1-1 1/2 lbs asparagus (for side)
  - 2 Tbsp olive oil (for asparagus)
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 425F. Toss asparagus with 2 Tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper on foil-lined sheet; set aside.
  - Pound chicken to even thickness. Season; dredge lightly in flour.
  - Heat 3 Tbsp olive oil in large skillet. Sear chicken 4-5 min/side until 165F. Remove.
  - Lower heat; add garlic (and red pepper flakes); cook 30-60 seconds.
  - Add chicken stock; scrape bits; simmer 1-2 min.
  - Stir in cream + Parmesan; simmer 2-3 min until thickened.
  - Add sun-dried tomatoes + spinach; cook until spinach wilts.
  - Return chicken; warm 1-2 min, spooning sauce over.
  - Roast asparagus 12-15 min while sauce simmers.
  - Serve chicken with sauce and asparagus.
tips:
  - One-skillet chicken + one sheet-pan asparagus.
  - Sauce clings best to pounded cutlets.
