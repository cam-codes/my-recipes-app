---
title: Creamy Wild Mushroom Risotto with Parmesan and Truffle Oil
description: Silky arborio rice loaded with sauteed wild mushrooms, finished with butter, Parmesan, and a luxurious truffle oil drizzle--one-pot elegance with earthy depth.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 40
estimatedCost: 24
ingredients:
  - 1 1/2 cups arborio rice
  - 1 1/2-2 lbs mixed wild mushrooms (shiitake, oyster, cremini, etc.)
  - 1 medium onion, finely diced
  - 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup dry white wine
  - 6-7 cups hot low-sodium chicken or vegetable stock
  - 4 Tbsp unsalted butter (divided)
  - 3/4-1 cup freshly grated Parmesan
  - Extra-virgin olive oil
  - 1-2 tsp truffle oil (for finishing)
  - Salt and black pepper
instructions:
  - Clean and slice/tear mushrooms. Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil + 1 Tbsp butter in wide pot over medium-high. Cook mushrooms in batches until golden (3-4 min undisturbed, then toss). Season; remove.
  - In same pot, add 1 Tbsp olive oil if needed. Cook onion + pinch salt 4-5 min until translucent. Add garlic 30-60 seconds.
  - Add arborio rice; stir 1-2 min until grains translucent at edges.
  - Pour in white wine; stir until absorbed.
  - Add hot stock 1 ladle at a time (~3/4 cup), stirring frequently, 18-22 minutes total until creamy and al dente.
  - Stir in mushrooms. Remove from heat.
  - Add 2 Tbsp cold butter + Parmesan; stir vigorously for creaminess. Adjust with stock if too thick.
  - Finish with truffle oil drizzle and extra Parmesan.
tips:
  - One-pot method. Keep stock hot for even cooking.
  - Texture should be loose enough to spread on plate.
