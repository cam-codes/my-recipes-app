---
title: Chicken Piccata with Green Beans
description: Bright, lemony pan-seared chicken cutlets in a buttery caper-wine sauce, paired with crisp garlicky green beans for a fresh, tangy, restaurant-style dinner in about 45 minutes.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 30
estimatedCost: 22
ingredients:
  - 1½–2 lbs boneless chicken breasts (cut into cutlets)
  - Salt and black pepper
  - All-purpose flour (~½ cup)
  - 3–4 Tbsp olive oil
  - 4 Tbsp unsalted butter (divided)
  - ½ cup dry white wine
  - ½ cup low-sodium chicken stock
  - Juice of 2–3 lemons + zest of 1
  - 4 Tbsp capers, drained
  - 2–3 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
  - Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  - 1–1½ lbs green beans, trimmed
  - 2 Tbsp unsalted butter (for beans)
  - "Optional: lemon squeeze for beans"
instructions:
  - Pound chicken to ½-inch thickness. Season; dredge in flour.
  - Heat 2–3 Tbsp olive oil in skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken 3–4 min/side. Remove.
  - Add 2 Tbsp butter + garlic (if using); cook 30 seconds.
  - Deglaze with wine; simmer 2–3 minutes.
  - Add stock + lemon juice; simmer 3–4 minutes.
  - Stir in capers, lemon zest, remaining butter until glossy.
  - Return chicken; warm 1–2 minutes.
  - "For beans: Blanch in boiling salted water 3–4 min. Drain; sauté in 2 Tbsp butter + garlic 2–3 min. Season."
  - Serve chicken with sauce and green beans. Garnish with parsley.
tips:
  - Bright, tangy sauce pairs well with crisp green beans.
  - Pound cutlets thin for quick, even cooking.
---
