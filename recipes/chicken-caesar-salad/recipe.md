---
title: Chicken Caesar Salad
description: Grilled lemony chicken atop crisp romaine, homemade garlicky croutons, and a perfectly emulsified anchovy-lemon dressing, finished with shaved Parmesan--restaurant-quality in one bowl.
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 20
estimatedCost: 26
ingredients:
  - 4 boneless chicken breasts (or 6-8 thighs)
  - 1/2 loaf day-old country bread or baguette, torn into chunks
  - 3 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 lemon
  - 1 clove garlic
  - 1 egg yolk (coddled 1 min in boiling water if concerned)
  - 2 anchovy fillets (or 1 tsp paste)
  - 2 cloves garlic, grated
  - 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  - 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  - 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  - 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  - 1/3 cup neutral oil + 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil (combined)
  - 3-4 hearts of romaine, chopped or torn
  - Extra shaved Parmesan (use vegetable peeler)
instructions:
  - "**Croutons:** Toss torn bread with olive oil, lemon zest, minced garlic, salt. Bake at 400F for 12-15 minutes on foil-lined sheet pan."
  - "**Chicken:** Season with salt, pepper, olive oil, lemon juice. Grill or pan-sear 5-6 minutes per side. Rest, then slice."
  - "**Dressing:** In large salad bowl, mash anchovy + garlic + pinch salt into paste. Whisk in egg yolk, Dijon, lemon juice, Worcestershire, Parmesan. Slowly drizzle in combined oils while whisking until creamy and emulsified."
  - "**Assembly:** Add chopped romaine to the same bowl; toss with dressing. Top with sliced chicken, croutons, and shaved Parmesan."
tips:
  - Make dressing in the serving bowl for zero extra dishes. 
  - Croutons and chicken can cook simultaneously.
