---
title: Ed's Korean Beef Bowls with Soft-Boiled Eggs
description: Quick, flavorful ground beef stir-fried in a savory-sweet coconut aminos sauce with garlic and ginger, served over rice with fresh veggies, jammy soft-boiled eggs, and a drizzle of extra sauce-simple, balanced, and elevated weeknight comfort.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 20
estimatedCost: 18
ingredients:
  - 1 lb ground beef (85/15 or 90/10)
  - 1 tbsp avocado oil or olive oil
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated (or 1 tsp ground ginger)
  - 1/3 cup coconut aminos
  - 1-2 tbsp honey or maple syrup (to taste)
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
  - 3 tbsp coconut aminos (for drizzle sauce)
  - 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (for drizzle sauce)
  - 1 tsp rice vinegar or lime juice (for drizzle sauce)
  - 1/2 tsp sesame oil (for drizzle sauce)
  - Optional: 1 tsp sriracha (for drizzle sauce)
  - Microwave jasmine or white rice (enough for 4 servings, TJ's or similar)
  - 1 cup shredded carrots
  - 1 cup cucumber, thinly sliced or diced
  - 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  - Sesame seeds (optional garnish)
  - 4 large eggs (for soft-boiled/jammy)
instructions:
  - "Cook rice according to package directions (microwave or stovetop). Fluff and keep warm."
  - "Whisk together drizzle sauce: 3 tbsp coconut aminos, 1 tbsp honey/maple, 1 tsp rice vinegar/lime, 1/2 tsp sesame oil, optional sriracha. Set aside."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp oil in large skillet over medium-high. Add ground beef; cook, breaking it up, 5-6 minutes until mostly browned. Drain excess fat if needed."
  - "Add garlic and ginger to beef. Cook 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Stir in 1/3 cup coconut aminos, 1-2 tbsp honey/maple, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, and optional red pepper flakes. Simmer 2-3 minutes until glossy and slightly thickened. Taste and adjust sweetness/saltiness."
  - "Soft-boiled eggs: Bring pot of water to gentle boil. Carefully lower eggs in. Boil 6 1/2-7 minutes for jammy yolks. Transfer to ice bath 5 minutes. Peel and halve."
  - "Assemble bowls: Rice on bottom, spoon Korean beef over rice, add shredded carrots and cucumber, drizzle with coconut amino sauce, top with halved soft-boiled egg, green onions, and sesame seeds."
tips:
  - Start eggs when beef is browning so everything finishes together.
  - Make drizzle sauce ahead-flavors meld nicely.
  - Optional add-ons can include avocado slices, kimchi, pickled red onions, or extra sriracha/chili crisp.
