---
title: Classic Apple Cinnamon Crumble
description: Tender, cinnamon-spiced apples baked beneath a buttery, crunchy oat crumble topping-cozy, comforting, and perfect with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
collection: sweet
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 45
estimatedCost: 12
ingredients:
  - Apple Filling: 6-7 medium apples (about 2.5-3 lbs / 1.1-1.4 kg), peeled, cored, and sliced (Granny Smith, Honeycrisp, or a mix)
  - Apple Filling: 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
  - Apple Filling: 1/4 cup (55g) packed light or dark brown sugar
  - Apple Filling: 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  - Apple Filling: 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  - Apple Filling: 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)
  - Apple Filling: 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  - Apple Filling: 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  - Apple Filling: Pinch of salt
  - Crumble Topping: 1 cup (125g) all-purpose flour
  - Crumble Topping: 3/4 cup (65g) old-fashioned rolled oats
  - Crumble Topping: 1/2 cup (110g) packed light or dark brown sugar
  - Crumble Topping: 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
  - Crumble Topping: 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  - Crumble Topping: 1/4 teaspoon salt
  - Crumble Topping: 1/2 cup (115g / 1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F (175C). Lightly grease a 9x9-inch (or similar) baking dish."
  - "In a large bowl, toss the sliced apples with granulated sugar, brown sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg (if using), lemon juice, vanilla, and salt until evenly coated."
  - "Transfer the apple mixture to the prepared baking dish and spread evenly."
  - "In a medium bowl, combine flour, oats, brown sugar, granulated sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Pour in melted butter and mix with a fork until coarse crumbs form."
  - "Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the apples, covering completely."
  - "Bake for 40-50 minutes, until topping is golden brown and apples are bubbling. (Tent with foil if topping browns too quickly.)"
  - "Let rest 10-15 minutes before serving to allow filling to thicken."
tips:
  - "For extra crunch, mix 1/2 cup chopped pecans or walnuts into the topping."
  - "Make ahead: Assemble (without baking) up to 24 hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate."
  - "Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3-4 days; reheat at 325F for 10-15 minutes to re-crisp the topping."
  - "Best served warm with vanilla ice cream, whipped cream, or custard."
---
