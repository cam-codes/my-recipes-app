---
title: Chicken Parmesan Meatballs
description: Juicy ground chicken meatballs simmered in marinara, topped with melty mozzarella—serve over pasta or in subs for a fun twist on classic chicken parm.
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 25
estimatedCost: 20
ingredients:
  - 1 lb ground chicken
  - ½ cup breadcrumbs
  - ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  - 1 egg
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  - Salt and pepper
  - 2 cups marinara sauce
  - 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  - "Optional: fresh basil"
  - "Serve with: pasta or sub rolls"
instructions:
  - Mix chicken, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, egg, garlic, seasoning, salt, pepper. Form into 1–1½ inch meatballs.
  - In large skillet, brown meatballs on all sides (~5–7 min). Remove if needed.
  - Add marinara to skillet; nestle meatballs in sauce.
  - Simmer covered 10–12 min until cooked through (internal 165°F).
  - Top with mozzarella; cover 1–2 min to melt.
  - Garnish with basil; serve.
tips:
  - Can bake at 400°F for 20 min instead of pan-frying.
  - Use in subs or over spaghetti.
---
