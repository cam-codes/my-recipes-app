---
title: Braised Bone-In Lamb Shanks with Red Wine & Root Vegetables
description: Tender bone-in lamb shanks slow-braised in red wine with aromatic vegetables, herbs, and a rich sauce, served with caramelized root vegetables-cozy, restaurant-quality comfort that tastes even better the next day.
prepTime: 25
cookTime: 180
estimatedCost: 55
ingredients:
  - 3-3.5 bone-in lamb shanks (about 2.5-3 lbs total)
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - All-purpose flour (~1/3-1/2 cup, for dredging)
  - 2-3 Tbsp olive oil
  - 2 medium onions, finely diced
  - 3-4 medium carrots, finely diced
  - 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  - 8-10 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup dry red wine
  - 1 (14-15 oz) can diced tomatoes
  - 3-4 cups low-sodium beef stock
  - 3-4 sprigs fresh rosemary
  - 3-4 sprigs fresh thyme
  - 2 bay leaves
  - 2.5-3 lbs mixed root vegetables (parsnips, turnips, extra carrots, potatoes; peeled and cut into chunks)
  - Optional: 1-2 Tbsp balsamic vinegar (for sauce depth)
instructions:
  - "Pat shanks dry; season generously with salt and pepper. Dredge lightly in flour, shaking off excess."
  - "Heat 2-3 Tbsp olive oil in large Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown shanks on all sides (4-5 min/side, in batches). Remove to plate."
  - "Reduce heat to medium. Add onions, carrots, celery, pinch salt. Cook 6-8 min until softened."
  - "Add garlic; cook 1 min. Pour in wine; scrape bits. Simmer 2-3 min until reduced by half."
  - "Stir in tomatoes, 3 cups stock, rosemary, thyme, bay leaves. Nestle shanks back in (liquid halfway up; add more stock if needed)."
  - "Cover and braise in 325F oven 2.5-3 hours until fork-tender. (Pressure cooker alternative: high 50-55 min + natural release 15-20 min.)"
  - "During last 45-60 min: Toss root vegetables with 2 Tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper. Roast on foil-lined sheet at 425F 35-45 min until caramelized."
  - "Remove shanks; tent. Skim fat from sauce. Simmer on stovetop 10-15 min to thicken. Optional: stir in balsamic."
  - "Serve shanks with roasted root vegetables and plenty of sauce spooned over."
tips:
  - Make ahead: Braise fully today; flavors deepen overnight. Reheat covered at 325F 25-35 min.
  - Root vegetables reheat crisp on sheet pan at 400F 10-15 min.
---
